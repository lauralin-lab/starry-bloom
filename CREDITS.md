# Starry Bloom — credits and licenses

## Painting
The Starry Night (1889), Vincent van Gogh. The underlying painting is in the public domain. The displayed image was supplied by the site owner. Animated star and sky effects are applied at runtime.

## Other music
Moonlight Reverie is an original generated piano composition, not Beethoven's Moonlight Sonata. The original improvisation and its piano arrangement were provided and developed by the site owner.

## Uploads and privacy
User-selected files are decoded and analysed in the visitor's browser. They are not transmitted to GitHub or an audio-analysis service. Files are temporary and disappear on page reload. Note detection is approximate, especially for polyphonic or vocal recordings. GitHub Pages receives normal requests to serve the website assets.
